Teterboro Airport · Private Aviation

Teterboro Car Service for Private Flights.

Ground transportation for private aviation at Teterboro Airport in New Jersey, about 12 miles from Midtown Manhattan. We work with your FBO so the car is staged and ready when the aircraft arrives.

We Work With Your FBO

Teterboro runs on FBOs, not terminals. We pick up and drop off at all of them, including Atlantic Aviation, Signature Aviation, Jet Aviation and Meridian. We contact the FBO desk directly before your arrival to confirm where the car should wait, so you are not standing outside looking for your driver.

Flight Times Change. We Plan for It.

Private flights move earlier and later all the time. Give us the tail number and we follow the flight. If the departure slips or the aircraft lands early, your chauffeur adjusts. You do not need to call us with updates.

Booking a Teterboro Pickup Online

  • Under Service Type, choose Point-to-Point.
  • For pick-up, type the name of your FBO, for example Atlantic Aviation or Signature, and select it from the list.
  • Add your tail number in the comments at the last step.

Rates for Teterboro are flat and confirmed when you book. Call the desk at 833-533-6773 if you want a price before you book or have a schedule that may change.

Beyond Teterboro

We also handle private aviation at Morristown Municipal Airport (MMU) and Long Island MacArthur (ISP), plus commercial flights at JFK, LaGuardia and Newark. For a full day of meetings after you land, the car can stay with you on an hourly basis. See corporate and roadshow transportation.

Discretion and Security

Many of our Teterboro clients are principals who expect privacy. Chauffeurs sign a confidentiality standard and do not discuss who they drive. When a trip calls for protection, our licensed security partner plans the transport and the detail together. Armored vehicles are available on request.

Common Questions

Which FBOs at Teterboro do you serve?

All of them, including Atlantic Aviation, Signature Aviation, Jet Aviation and Meridian. We confirm the staging spot with the FBO before your arrival.

How far is Teterboro from Manhattan?

Teterboro Airport is about 12 miles from Midtown Manhattan. Travel time depends on the bridge or tunnel and the time of day.

What if my private flight is delayed?

Send us the tail number and we follow the flight. Your chauffeur adjusts to the new arrival time.

Do you offer security for Teterboro arrivals?

Yes. Executive protection is provided by our licensed security partner and planned together with the transport. Armored vehicles are available on request.
